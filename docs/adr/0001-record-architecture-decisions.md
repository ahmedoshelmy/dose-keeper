# 1. Record architecture decisions

Date: 2026-11-20

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Design choices in this repo (serverless host, orchestration style, storage) are the interesting part and should be explainable later.

## Decision
Keep short Architecture Decision Records in `docs/adr`, numbered, in the Nygard format (context, decision, consequences).

## Consequences
Every structural change adds or supersedes an ADR. ADRs are cheap; reviewers read them before the code.
